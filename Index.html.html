Hindsight Past Paper Trial: How We Use Your Answers

Your answers are anonymous. We never ask for your name, and we cannot tell which paper is yours. This page explains what happens to the A-level Biology B past paper you hand in for the Hindsight trial. Please read it before you decide whether to hand in your paper.

Who we are

Hindsight is run by Reuben Wand with Waaiz Sadiq. It is a revision tool that finds the mistakes students keep making in exam answers.

For any questions, email roo@marburystudios.co.uk.

What we collect

We collect only your written answers and the questions they answer. Nothing else.

Before you hand in your paper, make sure it has none of these on it:
  • your name, initials or signature
  • your candidate number or student number
  • your school, class or teacher's name
  • anything else about you, inside your answers too

We do not record who handed in which paper, and there is no consent form to sign. We type up each paper and then destroy the paper copy, so your handwriting is not kept.

Why we use it

We use the answers to build and test Hindsight:

  1. Testing the marking. Each answer is marked automatically against the exam board mark scheme, and the reasons marks were lost are recorded.
  2. Checking the marking. Reuben and Waaiz also mark answers by hand, to see whether the automatic marking agrees with a person.
  3. Improving Hindsight. We look at the kinds of mistakes students make so we can explain them better.

Your answers are not graded, not given to your school and not used to train AI models. Nothing is sold or shared with advertisers.

Taking part is optional. If you would rather not, just don't hand in your paper. Nothing happens either way.

Who else sees it

Anthropic may not train models on Customer Content from Services. (Commercial Terms, section B, checked 19 September 2026)

Answers go to Anthropic Ireland Limited and then to the United States for marking.

Before an answer is sent, it is checked for names, email addresses, phone numbers and postcodes. Anything we find is removed first.

How long we keep it

We keep the typed-up answers and their marks until 31 August 2027, the end of the 2026/27 school year, then delete them, including backups. Paper copies are destroyed as soon as they have been typed up.

Changing your mind

Decide before you hand in your paper. Because there is no name on it, we cannot find your paper afterwards to give it back or delete it.

If you realise you wrote something personal in an answer, email roo@marburystudios.co.uk and describe what you wrote. We will look for it and remove it.
